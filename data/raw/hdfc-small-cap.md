# HDFC Small Cap Fund

Direct-Growth. Category: Small Cap.
Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund-direct-growth

## Expense ratio
Expense ratio: 0.78%. Base expense ratio: 0.63%.

## Exit load
Exit load of 1% if redeemed within 1 year.

## Minimum SIP
Minimum SIP: 100. Minimum lump-sum investment: 100.

## Lock-in
Lock-in: No lock-in is stated on this page.

## Riskometer
Riskometer: Very High.

## Benchmark
Benchmark: BSE 250 SmallCap Total Return Index (BSE 250 SmallCap TRI).

## Tax
Tax: If you redeem within one year, returns are taxed at 20%. If you redeem after one year, returns exceeding Rs 1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at 12.5%.

## Overview
The scheme seeks to provide long-term capital appreciation /income by investing predominantly in Small-Cap companies.
